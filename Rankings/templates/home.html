This site publishes Chittagong Board (BISE-CTG) SSC and HSC exam result rankings for Bangladesh. It covers the Chittagong board only — it does not cover Dhaka or any other education board. Results are collected from the board's public result portal, and the board's own published result is always authoritative if there is ever a discrepancy.

The education boards themselves do not publish merit positions. The ranks shown here are computed independently by this site, from each student's total marks across all subjects, for every group: Science, Commerce (Business Studies), and Arts (Humanities). Students who score the same total marks share the same rank. Because ranks are computed only from roll numbers that were successfully retrieved from the board's portal, coverage for any given exam set may be incomplete. See the Methodology page for full details on how this works, what data is stored, and how to request removal of your information.

Three exam sets are currently available: HSC 2024, HSC 2025, and SSC 2025. Each is broken down into three group rankings. Use the links below to jump directly to any group and exam set, or search by roll number from any results page once you're viewing a group.
