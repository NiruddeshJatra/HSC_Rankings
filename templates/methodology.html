Methodology & Disclaimer

Independent and unofficial

This site is independent and unofficial. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Chattogram, or the Ministry of Education.

Data source

Data is collected from the board's public result portal. The board's own published result is authoritative and prevails in any discrepancy.

How ranks are computed

The education boards do not publish merit positions. Ranks shown on this site are computed by this site from total marks. Students with equal total marks share the same rank. Only roll numbers successfully retrieved from the board's portal are included, so coverage may be incomplete.

Coverage

This site covers the Chittagong (Chattogram) board only.

Privacy

Published pages show a student's roll number, name, group, rank, board, session, result, GPA, institute, and subject marks. Parents' names and registration numbers are not shown publicly, and individual result pages are excluded from search engine indexing.

To request removal of your information, email nasif@niruddeshjatra.space.
